## 📊 Project Overview

Pricing is a critical factor in the success of any café. Setting prices too high can reduce demand, while pricing too low can reduce profitability.

This project applies **data analytics and statistical modeling** to determine optimal menu prices that balance **customer demand and revenue generation**.

The workflow demonstrates a complete **data analytics pipeline** including data preparation, exploratory analysis, and regression-based price optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main goals of this project are:

- Analyze sales and customer demand patterns  
- Understand the relationship between **price and sales volume**  
- Build a regression model to estimate demand  
- Identify **optimal pricing strategies** for café products

---

## 🔎 Exploratory Data Analysis

EDA is used to understand product performance and sales behavior.

Key insights explored:

- Sales trends across menu items  
- Customer purchasing patterns  
- Impact of price on demand  
- Revenue contribution of each product

---

## 🤖 Model

The project uses **Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression** to model the relationship between:

- Product Price  
- Sales Volume  
- Revenue  

The model helps estimate how **price changes influence customer demand**.
